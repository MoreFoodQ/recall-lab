LLM Hallucination
• When used to generate text, language models are designed 
to make things up. That’s all they do. But since they only 
ever make things up, when the text they have extruded 
happens to be interpretable as something we deem correct, 
that is by chance.
• There’s a balance between creativity and perfect accuracy, 
and the model will need to learn when you want one or the 
other.
