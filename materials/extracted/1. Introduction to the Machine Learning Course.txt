Final Project Guidelines
• Topic: No restriction on the topic, but it must be related to Machine Learning.
• Requirements: Your project should include:
–A clear data source (can be publicly available or self-collected)
–Data analysis and preprocessing
–Model training
–Performance evaluation
• Team Formation: 2–3 students per group.
• Deliverables:
–Demo Video showcasing your project
–Final Project Presentation: 10 minutes + 2 minutes Q&A
